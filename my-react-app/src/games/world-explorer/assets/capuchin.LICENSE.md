## Changes made for the game (CC BY 4.0 asks that these be stated)

- The original is a realistic seated **rhesus macaque**, an Old World monkey of Asia; there are no
  macaques in the Amazon. It is **re-skinned as a capuchin** (a New World monkey of the rainforest with
  a similar seated build and size) by a shader (rendering/amazonCapuchinCoat.js): dark brown fur
  (keeping the model's own fur shading), a cream face frame, throat and upper chest, a dark cap, and dark
  hands, feet and ears. The face, eyes and nose keep the model's own painting. It is a macaque's shape
  wearing a capuchin's coat, so it is an approximation of a capuchin, not an exact model of one.
- Converted from the old specular-glossiness material to a standard one; reduced from 35,000 to about
  12,300 triangles (a simplified version); the colour texture reduced to a 1024 x 1024 JPEG and the
  other maps dropped.
- Centred and set with its feet on y = 0, facing +z.

The original file was 3.6 MB; this one is 419 KB.
