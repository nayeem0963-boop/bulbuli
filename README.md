# Bulbuli 🐦

> Learn to code, one tiny question at a time.

Bulbuli is a code-learning platform built around a simple idea: **small steps, quick wins, real progress.** Instead of long lectures, you learn through a rapid back-and-forth of bite-sized questions and answers — a binary rhythm that keeps your brain engaged and your momentum high.

---

## ✨ How It Works

Every concept is broken down into **micro Q&A pairs**. You read a short prompt, answer it, and instantly see whether you got it right. No walls of text. No passive video watching. Just active learning, one question at a time.
